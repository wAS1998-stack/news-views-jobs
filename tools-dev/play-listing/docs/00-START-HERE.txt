STEP BY STEP IN PLAY CONSOLE
----------------------------
(Open play.google.com/console and click your app "Sarkari Radar". Menu names can differ a little.)

A. MAIN STORE LISTING   (left menu: Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing)
   1. App name ............ paste 4-store-text/1-app-name.txt
   2. Short description ... paste 4-store-text/2-short-description.txt
   3. Full description .... paste 4-store-text/3-full-description.txt
   4. App icon ............ upload 1-app-icon/icon-512.png
   5. Feature graphic ..... upload 2-feature-graphic/feature-graphic-1024x500.jpg
   6. Video ............... leave empty
   7. Phone screenshots ... upload all 8 files from 3-phone-screenshots, in order 01 to 08
                            (the first 3 appear in Play search results, so keep this order)
   8. 7-inch tablet screenshots .... upload all the files in 3b-tablet-7-inch-screenshots, in number order
      10-inch tablet screenshots ... upload all the files in 3c-tablet-10-inch-screenshots, in number order
      (real screens of the app on tablet-size displays; Chromebook / TV / Wear: leave empty)
   9. Click Save.

B. STORE SETTINGS   (Grow users > Store presence > Store settings)
   Category: Education. Contact details: see 5-store-settings-and-app-content.txt

C. APP CONTENT   (Policy and programs > App content, or "Dashboard > Set up your app")
   Answer each item with 5-store-settings-and-app-content.txt

D. RELEASE NOTES   (when you create the release in Testing > Closed testing)
   In the "Release notes" box write:
   <en-IN>
   ...paste 4-store-text/4-whats-new-release-notes.txt here...
   </en-IN>

E. Click "Send for review" (Publishing overview). Google's first review usually takes a few days.


DO NOT CHANGE THESE WHEN YOU EDIT THE LISTING (Play rules)
---------------------------------------------------------
- No "best", "No.1", "#1", "top", "free", "new" or emojis in the app name or in the graphics.
- Keep the "not affiliated with any government" lines and the list of official websites in the full
  description. Google requires both for apps that show government information.
- Screenshots must show the real app, without ads. These do.
- Don't add lists of keywords or names of other apps (Sarkari Result, Testbook, etc.). Keyword stuffing
  gets listings rejected. The keywords are already written in naturally.
- Don't promise anything the app doesn't do.


FILE RULES (already met, for your information)
---------------------------------------------
Icon: 512 x 512, 32-bit PNG. Feature graphic: 1024 x 500, JPEG, no transparency.
Screenshots: JPEG, no transparency, 9:16 portrait. Phone and 7-inch tablet 1080 x 1920, 10-inch
tablet 1440 x 2560 (Play wants each side between 1,080 and 7,680 px for 10-inch). 1080 px or more and
at least 4 screenshots is what Google asks for when choosing apps to recommend.
