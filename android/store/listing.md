# Google Play listing — Sarkari Radar (app by News-Views)

**App name (30):** Sarkari Radar: Govt Job Alerts

**Hindi listing name:** सरकारी रडार: नौकरी अलर्ट · **Developer name:** News-Views (website news-views.in)

**Short description (80):** Sarkari naukri alerts for your state & qualification, plus results & admit cards

**Full description:**

Independent app — not affiliated with any government body. Job details come from official recruitment notifications and government websites, which are linked on every job.

Sarkari Radar brings every new government job notification to your phone — and only the ones that fit you.

Pick your state and your highest qualification once. When a matching recruitment is announced, you get an alert with the number of posts and the last date. All-India central jobs (SSC, Railway, banks, defence, PSUs) always come through.

WHAT YOU GET
• New government jobs from central departments, every state, PSUs, universities and courts — updated around the clock
• Filters: state, qualification (10th, 12th, Diploma/ITI, Graduate, Engineering, Post Graduate) and "closing soon"
• Clear job pages: posts, last date, age limit, qualification, fee, salary, selection process, how to apply and common questions
• Direct links to the official notification and the official website for every job
• Results, admit cards and answer keys of major exams, with download steps
• Upcoming dates: exams, admit cards and results in date order
• Follow a department (SSC, RRB, UPSC, your state PSC…) and get everything it announces
• "Fits my age" filter and an age check on every job, with OBC / SC / ST relaxation
• Browse by category: Railway, Bank, SSC, Police, Defence, Teaching, Medical, PSU and more
• Save jobs and get reminders 2 days before the last date and on the last day
• Hindi and English — switch any time
• Works offline with the last data you loaded; small and fast

SOURCES AND DISCLAIMER
Sarkari Radar is an independent app by News-Views (news-views.in). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any government body. Job details are taken from official recruitment notifications and the official websites of the recruiting organisations (for example ssc.gov.in, indianrailways.gov.in, upsc.gov.in and state public service commission websites); each listing links to its official source. Always confirm the details on the official website before applying. Sarkari Radar never asks for fees or personal documents.
