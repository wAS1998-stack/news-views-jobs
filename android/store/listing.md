# Google Play listing — Sarkari Radar (app by News-Views)

**App name (30):** Sarkari Radar: Govt Job Alerts

**Hindi listing name:** सरकारी रडार: नौकरी अलर्ट · **Developer name:** News-Views (website news-views.in)

**Short description (80):** Sarkari naukri alerts for your state & qualification, plus results & admit cards

**Full description (en-IN):**

Independent app — not affiliated with any government body. Every listing links to its official notification or the recruiting organisation's website; always confirm the details there before you apply.

Sarkari Radar brings new government job notifications to your phone — and only the ones that fit you.

Pick your state and your highest qualification once. When a matching recruitment is announced, you get an alert with the number of posts and the last date. Central government jobs (SSC, Railway, banks, defence, PSUs) come through too, and an evening summary lists the day's biggest new jobs for your state.

WHAT YOU GET
• "For you": the Jobs tab opens on jobs for your states and qualification — all jobs are one tap away
• New sarkari naukri from central departments, every state, PSUs, universities and courts, updated around the clock
• Clear job pages: posts, last date, age limit, qualification, fee, salary, selection process and how to apply
• Results, admit cards and answer keys of major exams, with the exam or result date and a "Remind me" bell
• Daily current affairs quiz with explanations and a streak — two minutes a day for SSC, Railway, Bank and state exams
• Search that understands states, qualifications and Hindi: "UP police 12th", "रेलवे", "bank graduate"
• Follow a department (SSC, RRB, UPSC, your state PSC…) and get everything it announces
• Save jobs: reminders 2 days before the last date and on the last day, a "New last date" alert if it moves, and saved jobs work offline
• "Fits my age" check with OBC / SC / ST relaxation
• Railway, Bank, Police, Defence, Teaching, Medical and PSU bharti in one place
• Hindi and English — switch any time; small and fast

SOURCES AND DISCLAIMER
Sarkari Radar is an independent app by News-Views (news-views.in). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any government body. Listings are compiled from official recruitment notifications, government websites and public job notices, and each one links to its official source (for example ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, indianrailways.gov.in and state public service commission websites). Always confirm the details on the official website before applying. Sarkari Radar never asks for fees or personal documents. Found a mistake? Use "Report a mistake" on any job.
