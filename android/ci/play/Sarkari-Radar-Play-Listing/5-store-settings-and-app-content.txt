DATA SAFETY   (what the app really does)
-----------
Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?  Yes
Is all of the data encrypted in transit?  Yes
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?  No
   (the app has no accounts; settings and saved jobs stay on the phone)
Account creation: the app doesn't let users create an account.

Declare these data types (tick "Collected"; tick "Shared" where it says so):

Location > Approximate location
   Collected and shared (ads SDK, from the IP address; Firebase Analytics region)
   Purposes: Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention, security and compliance
   Required (users can't turn it off)

App activity > App interactions
   Collected and shared (ads SDK: ad views and taps; Firebase Analytics: screens and buttons)
   Purposes: Analytics, Advertising or marketing
   Required

App info and performance > Crash logs
   Collected, not shared (Firebase Crashlytics)
   Purposes: App functionality, Analytics
   Required

App info and performance > Diagnostics
   Collected and shared (ads SDK)
   Purposes: Analytics, Fraud prevention, security and compliance, Advertising or marketing
   Required

Device or other IDs
   Collected and shared (advertising ID / app set ID for ads; Firebase ID for notifications)
   Purposes: App functionality, Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention, security and compliance
   Required

Do NOT declare: name, email, phone, contacts, photos, messages, precise location, files, health,
financial info. The app doesn't collect them.
(If you switch on the one-time "Pro" purchase later, add Financial info > Purchase history, collected
by Firebase Analytics for Analytics, and change "Digital purchases" in the content rating to Yes.)
