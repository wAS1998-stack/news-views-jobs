Sarkari Radar brings new sarkari naukri to your phone: only the govt jobs that fit your state and qualification, each with its official notification one tap away.

Independent app, not affiliated with any government body. Every job links to its official source, so you can check the details there before you apply.

Pick your state and highest qualification once. When a matching government job is announced, you get a job alert with the number of posts and the last date. Central government jobs (SSC, Railway, Bank, Defence, UPSC and PSU recruitment) come through too. Hundreds of new vacancies are added every week, around the clock.

WHY STUDENTS USE SARKARI RADAR
• Govt jobs and vacancies for your state: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Gujarat, West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Assam and every other state
• Jobs by qualification: 10th pass, 12th pass, ITI / diploma, graduate and post-graduate
• Clear job pages: posts, last date, age limit, qualification, fee, salary, selection process and how to apply
• "Fits my age" check, with OBC / SC / ST relaxation
• The apply button says what it opens: apply online, notice & form, walk-in notice or the official website
• Walk-in interviews marked clearly

RESULTS, ADMIT CARDS AND EXAM DATES
• Sarkari result, admit card, answer key and exam date updates for major exams
• Upcoming dates calendar: which exams, admit cards and results are due today, tomorrow and this week
• A "Remind me" bell on every date

NEVER MISS A LAST DATE
• Save a job: reminders 2 days before the last date and on the last day
• An alert when a last date is extended
• Follow a department (SSC, RRB, UPSC, IBPS, your state PSC or police board) and get everything it announces
• An evening summary of the day's biggest new jobs for your state
• Saved jobs open even without internet

DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS QUIZ
Two minutes a day: a new current affairs quiz every morning, with the right answer and a short explanation for each question, and a streak to keep you going. Useful practice for SSC, Railway, Bank, Police and state exams.

HINDI AND ENGLISH
Use the whole app in Hindi or English and switch any time. Search understands both: "UP police 12th", "railway", "रेलवे", "bank graduate".

ALL DEPARTMENTS IN ONE PLACE
Every kind of sarkari naukri in one place: railway jobs (RRB, RRC, metro), bank jobs (IBPS, SBI, RBI, NABARD, gramin banks), SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), UPSC and state PSC, police bharti, Army, Navy, Air Force and paramilitary forces, teacher recruitment (TET, KVS, NVS), medical and nursing (AIIMS, NHM, ESIC), PSU, court, post office, university and research jobs.

LIGHT AND FAST
No login, no sign-up. Quick on low-cost phones and slow networks.

SOURCES AND DISCLAIMER
Sarkari Radar is an independent app by News-Views (news-views.in). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any government body, and it is not a recruitment agency. Job details are compiled from official recruitment notifications, government websites and public job notices. Each listing links to its official source, for example:
• Staff Selection Commission: https://ssc.gov.in
