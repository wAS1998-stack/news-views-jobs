GROWTH PLAN - MORE ORGANIC INSTALLS FROM GOOGLE PLAY
=====================================================
Play ranks an app on: keywords in the listing, installs, ratings and reviews, how many people keep
the app (retention), crashes, and how many store visitors install. In that order of effort:


1. KEYWORDS - done in this listing
   The listing targets what Indian job seekers type in Play: sarkari naukri, govt job alert,
   government jobs, sarkari result, admit card, result, answer key, exam date, railway jobs, bank
   jobs, SSC, police bharti, teacher jobs, 10th / 12th pass jobs, ITI / diploma, current affairs quiz,
   and state names (Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan...).
   Google reads them in the app name (strongest), the short description and the full description.
   Leave them as they are; adding more repetitions or keyword lists hurts.

2. FIRST RATINGS (week 1-3)
   - Your 12+ closed testers: ask them to use the app for real and, once it is public, to leave an
     honest rating and a short review in their own words.
     Never pay for reviews, never offer anything for a rating, never ask for "5 stars only":
     Play removes such reviews and can suspend the app.
   - The app already shows Google's in-app review card at good moments: a quiz score of 7 or more,
     saving a second job, or coming back after opening an application page (Google limits how often).
   - Reply to every review within a day, most of all the 1-3 star ones. When you fix a problem,
     reply "Fixed in version X - please update". Ratings go up when people see answers.

3. YOUR WEBSITE = YOUR BIGGEST FREE INSTALL SOURCE (the day the app is live)
   Send me the Play link. I will set playUrl in config.json, so every news-views.in job page shows
   "Get the app on Google Play" and shared app links point to Play. Also send the App signing SHA-256
   (Play Console > Test and release > Setup > App signing) so news-views.in links open in the app.

4. STORE LISTING EXPERIMENTS (Play Console > Grow users > Store listing experiments)
   Start when about 1,000 people a week visit the store page. Test ONE thing at a time, 50/50, at
   least 7-14 days, then keep the winner:
   - Short description B:
     Govt job alerts for your state & qualification: results, admit cards, GK quiz   (77)
   - Short description C:
     New govt jobs for your state & qualification. Results, admit cards, daily quiz  (78)
   - First screenshot: try 08-no-login-quick-setup.jpg in position 1.
   - App name B:  Sarkari Radar: Sarkari Naukri   (29)

5. KEEP THE APP HEALTHY (Play Console > Monitor and improve > Android vitals)
   Play shows a warning on the store page and ranks the app lower when, over 28 days, more than
   1.09% of daily users see a crash or more than 0.47% see an "app not responding". Crashlytics is
   built in: send me any crash and I fix it.

6. UPDATE EVERY 2-4 WEEKS
   Each update with a clear "What's new" helps ranking and brings people back. I prepare the builds;
   you upload them (or set up Play's automatic publishing later).

7. CHANNELS YOU POST IN YOURSELF
   Your Telegram channel (and a WhatsApp channel if you start one): post big new jobs with the Play
   link. I can't post on your behalf.

8. LATER, WHEN THERE IS BUDGET
   - A 20-30 second screen recording of the app on YouTube, added as the listing video.
   - Google Ads "App campaigns" for paid installs: start small (Rs 300-500 a day), watch cost per
     install and day-7 retention before spending more.
